Privacy Policy

Borderless Bites · Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Borderless Bites mobile application ("the app") handles information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location. The app has no login and no user profiles.

Some information is stored only on your own device and is never sent to us:

  • Recipes you mark as favourites
  • Your language preference
  • Cooking steps you have checked off

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Advertising

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process certain information, including a device advertising identifier, general device information, and app usage data. This processing is carried out by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can limit ad personalisation at any time through your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

The app is a general-audience cooking app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Internet access

The app requires an internet connection to load advertisements. Recipe content is included in the app itself.

Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party service, which may collect data as described in its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
